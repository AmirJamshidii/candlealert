How suspects are scored

Wallets are ranked by a Suspect Score combining four signals:
  • 30% — Signal appearances: how many reversal signals the wallet appeared in as a top position holder
  • 30% — BTC dominance ratio: share of their portfolio bet on BTC direction markets vs. other markets
  • 20% — Total PnL: cumulative profit across all matched signals
  • 20% — Total wagered: total USDC size of positions held at signal time
Each factor is normalised 0–1 across all candidates, then weighted and summed. Higher score = more suspicious.
